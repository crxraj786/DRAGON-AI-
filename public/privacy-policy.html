DRAGON AIPrivacy & data use

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

This policy explains how DRAGON AI handles information when you use its Android application or web service. The current app does not require an account.

1. Information you provide

When you send a prompt, the text you enter and the conversation context needed to answer it are sent to the DRAGON AI server. If you attach a supported text file, its included text is sent as part of your message. Do not submit passwords, payment details, government identifiers, or other sensitive information.

2. AI processing and service providers

To generate an answer, DRAGON AI may send your prompt and relevant conversation context to the AI model provider configured for the service. The current deployment is configured to use Google Gemini. The provider processes submitted content under its own terms and privacy policy. DRAGON AI does not place provider API keys in the Android app.

3. Conversation history and device storage

When chat saving is enabled, conversation history and app settings are stored locally on your device. The current app does not provide account-based cloud synchronization. You can export your data from Settings or delete local conversations in the app. Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored data.

4. Network and operational data

Requests pass through the hosting infrastructure used to operate the service. Infrastructure and AI providers may process technical information such as request metadata, IP address, timestamps, and security logs according to their own policies. The service applies request-size limits, timeouts, and basic rate limiting to protect availability.

5. Sharing and sale

We do not sell your conversation content. Content is shared with the AI provider only as needed to generate a response and may be processed by hosting infrastructure to deliver and protect the service. We do not currently offer advertising profiles or account-based data sharing.

6. Children

DRAGON AI is a general-purpose assistant and is not specifically designed for children. Do not use the service to submit a child's sensitive personal information.

7. Security and retention

Reasonable technical safeguards are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Local history remains on your device until you delete it or the app data is removed. Server and provider operational logs may be retained for periods determined by the relevant hosting or AI provider.

8. Your choices

  • Turn off local chat saving in Settings.
  • Export conversations before deleting them if you need a copy.
  • Avoid submitting confidential or sensitive information.
  • Stop using the service if you do not agree with this policy.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as app features and service providers change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the project through the DRAGON AI GitHub issue page. Do not post private personal information publicly; ask for a private contact route if needed.

DRAGON AI · This policy describes the currently implemented service, not planned features such as accounts, cloud history, or file analysis.
